What a tune-up actually covers.

A tune-up should take more than ten minutes and a new filter. Ours takes about an hour, and you get a written checklist when we leave.

  • Clean the condenser and evaporator coils
  • Check refrigerant pressure against the manufacturer's spec
  • Flush the condensate drain line so it can't back up
  • Tighten electrical connections and test the capacitor
  • Measure the temperature drop across the coil
  • Replace the filter and show you how to do it yourself
